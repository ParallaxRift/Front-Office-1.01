      Power Rankings

      Power blends roster strength (the value of each team’s best starting lineup plus its three best bench players) with title odds, which add record, injuries, points scored and trend. Results count for more as the season goes on.

      Season Simulator

      Simulates the rest of the regular season and playoffs 10,000 times, factoring in current records, player values, injuries, recent player scoring, each team's points scored and which way it's trending to estimate each team’s playoff and championship odds.

      Team Statuses

      The Contender, Middle and Rebuilding tags you see across Front Office, and how each team earns one.

      What the Team Statuses Mean

      Every team is ranked by roster strength using the same value score behind the Power Rankings, then the league is divided into three tiers: top, middle, and bottom third. During the season, each team's playoff odds from the Season Simulator count too, starting after Week 3 and growing to 60% of the score late in the season.

      Contender
      The strongest third of the league. Built to win now. Team Strategy and Trade Finder steer contenders toward proven starters and away from trading them for futures.
      Middle
      The middle third. Close enough to push for a title or start a retool. Strategy picks a direction from the roster's age and draft picks: young or pick-rich teams lean toward building, older ones toward pushing in.
      Rebuilding
      The weakest third. Better served building for future seasons. Recommendations favor young players and draft picks, and suggest selling veterans before they lose value.

      Statuses update automatically whenever rosters or values change. Early in the season they mostly describe roster strength, so a strong roster off to a slow start is still a Contender. As the season goes on, record matters more: a thin roster that keeps winning moves up, and a strong roster that keeps losing moves down.

        About Front Office

        Front Office is a dynasty fantasy football trade calculator built around your league. Sign in with your Sleeper username and every value, grade and suggestion is fitted to your league's rules, rosters and history, not a one-size-fits-all chart.

        Values Built for Your League

        • Rankings: every player and draft pick ranked for your league's format, lineup and scoring, with filters for position, rookies and injured players, and your own players highlighted.
        • League tuning: values learn from the trades your league actually makes, so they reflect how your league trades.
        • Draft picks: each pick is valued by where it's likely to land and the strength of its draft class.
        • Kept current: rankings are updated every week, and depth charts and injuries refresh several times a day.

        Trade Tools

        • Trade Calculator: build any trade between two teams and see who wins, how close it is, and what would even it out. Share it with a link or an image.
        • Trade Finder: pick a player to move and get fair offers that fit your team, or find realistic targets and what it would take to land them.
        • Trade History: every trade in your league graded with today's values, plus each team's trade record.

        Your Team

        • My Team: a game plan for contending or rebuilding, a position-by-position report card, buy and sell lists, and your best trade partners.
        • Player cards: tap any player for his depth chart spot, current injury status with an estimated return, and career stats.

        Your League

        • League: standings, power rankings, a season simulator with playoff and title odds, the Trophy Room of past champions, team statuses and league settings.
        • Scores: every matchup with box scores, refreshing on game days.
        • Two views: The Basics for a quick trade check, or Freakshow for every tool. See what each shows.
        • On your phone: add Front Office to your home screen and use it like an app.

        The Basics vs. Freakshow

        Front Office has two modes. Switch any time with the toggle on the right side of the league header. Your choice is remembered on each device.

        The Basics

        For newer players, or anyone who just wants to check a trade.

        What You Get

        • Trade Calculator: build any trade between two teams and see who wins, with the value adjustment and suggestions to even it out.
        • Rankings: every player and pick ranked for your league's settings.
        • League Settings: your league's format, lineup, scoring, and how they change values (from the button in the league header).

        Freakshow

        For managers who want every edge. This is the default.

        Everything in The Basics, Plus

        • Trade Finder (in Trade Tools): pick a player and get fair trades that fit your team's direction, or find realistic targets and what it would cost to land them.
        • My Team: a game plan, position report card, trade targets, sell list, and best trade partners.
        • Trade History (in Trade Tools): every league trade graded with today's values, team trade records, and the full trade log.
        • League: standings, power rankings, the season simulator, and what the team statuses mean.
        • Trophy Room (in League): past champions and weekly high points.
        • Scores: every matchup, refreshing every minute on game days.

        What Stays the Same

        • The values are identical in both modes. League settings, league tuning, and the value adjustment all work the same, so a trade grades the same way whichever mode you're in.
        • Nothing is lost when you switch. The Basics only hides tabs; switch back to Freakshow and everything is right where you left it.
        • Each person picks their own mode. It's saved in your browser, so your league mates can use whichever mode they like.

        Beta

        Sleeper Leagues in Beta

        Front Office currently works with Sleeper leagues only. Support for Yahoo, ESPN, and other platforms is slated for future development.

        Why Sleeper First

        Sleeper makes league data publicly available with nothing more than a username. That means Front Office can read your rosters, settings, traded picks, and live scores without asking for a password or a login, which made it the best place to build and test everything.

        What "Beta" Means

        • Everything works today for Sleeper leagues: league-specific values, the trade calculator, league settings, team strategy, and live scores.
        • Values are still being tuned. The league adjustments are starting estimates and will be refined as more real trade data comes in, so numbers may shift.
        • Features and layout may change as the tool grows.
        • Dynasty leagues come first. Redraft and keeper leagues load, but the values and strategy are built for dynasty.
